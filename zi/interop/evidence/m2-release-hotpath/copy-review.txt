Native has one additional EMPTY capacity-extension call per initial buffer:
its arena starts from pooled capacity, whereas canonical starts from zero
logical capacity and enters its allocation path. This adds no physical
allocation or copying relative to canonical. Empty extensions are reported
separately; raw total counts are retained, not silently normalized.

Scope: growth-path data movement only, excluding payload, scratch and scalar
writes. These representative measurements resolve the previously unavailable
copy counters; they do not establish nested-message or full application
allocation parity, nor timing acceptance. Debugger timings are excluded.
